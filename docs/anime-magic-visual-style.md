# Mushoku Tensei-inspired magic visuals

This is a gameplay-focused adaptation of representative anime spell scenes, not a frame-by-frame tracing of every episode. No anime screenshots or copied artwork are included in the mod.

## Visual cues translated into Minecraft particles

- **Fire:** the oversized fireball shown during the Counter Arrow encounter reads as a hot, layered mass: a pale-hot center, bright yellow-orange body, and red-orange outer flame. At very high output, a restrained violet fringe distinguishes an overcharged blast rather than recoloring ordinary fire.
- **Water:** the early Water Ball scenes emphasize a rounded, flowing projectile. The mod uses cyan and clear blue with white glints, layered over Minecraft's splash and bubble particles.
- **Ice:** a colder white/cyan/blue variant of water, with snowflake particles and bright icy highlights.
- **Earth:** muted stone, ash, and ochre tones accompany the existing rock/impact particles, rather than a saturated elemental glow.
- **Wind:** pale cyan and near-white accents sit over gust/cloud particles to suggest fast, translucent air currents.
- **Healing and light:** soft green-white healing glow with a warm gold highlight; light magic uses a brighter gold-white palette.
- **Repair:** warm gold with a small green accent, visually separated from healing.

The palette is applied to the casting trail and impact bursts using Minecraft's colorizable dust particles; the existing splash, flame, snowflake, gust, heart, smoke, and impact particles remain as shape and texture layers. The purple fire accent is only used for overcharged fire effects.

## Recognizable anime techniques

- **Water Cannon** is implemented as a long cyan-blue jet with a bright core, splash accents, damage, and knockback along its path; its width grows with magical power.
- **Cumulonimbus** creates a broad rain-cloud and falling-rain particle effect over the aimed area, slows creatures, and clears nearby fire. Minecraft's short-lived particle system is used instead of changing world weather.
- **Earth Hedgehog** raises a ring of ochre-and-stone particle spires around the aim point, damaging, slowing, and lifting nearby creatures without placing or breaking blocks.
- **Stone Cannon**, **Quagmire**, **Icicle Lance**, **Exodus Flame**, and **Nuclear Explosion** are additional casting phrases for related existing earth, water, and fire spells.

These are gameplay-oriented interpretations of recognizable spell names and visuals, not a complete or frame-accurate simulation of the anime.
